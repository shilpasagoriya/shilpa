About Me
Building for India's Digital Future

I'm a Full Stack Developer with 4 years of hands-on experience across government, healthcare, and logistics domains. My core expertise is Java Spring Boot backend engineering — designing REST APIs, building event-driven microservices, and integrating complex government platforms.

At NEGD (National E-Governance Division), I contribute to Sagar Setu / MSW 2.0 — India's national maritime single-window for the Indian Ports Association. I built the end-to-end Digital Signature card: QR-code generation, PDF stamping (PDFBox/iText), and AWS S3 integration.

I designed the iVTMS Vessel Zone Event API — real-time vessel tracking where NTCPWC pushes zone events via POST /ivtsevents/, routed securely via WSO2 API Gateway. Also integrated ULIP VAHAN & SARATHI APIs for vehicle and DL verification.

Beyond Java, I'm trained in SAP ABAP (Reports, DDIC, ALV, OOP ABAP, RICEF) and experienced across Angular, React, Kafka, IBM MQ, Docker, and AWS. I've led UAT testing cycles and coordinated new port onboardings.
